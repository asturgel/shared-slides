Phase 1 — Understand Together

Value Discovery

Core Question Who is our customer?

Phase 1 is where we build the foundation. Before we can decide what to do, we need a shared picture of who the customer is, what they care about, and what's at stake.

  • 1Build a shared customer understanding
  • 2Identify 4–6 core customer objectives
  • 3Validate priorities directly with the customer
  • 4Use AI to surface insights, then refine with team context

What good looks like in Discovery

By the end of Phase 1, your plan should pass these three tests.

✓
Objectives are validated by the customer, not assumed.
The 4–6 customer objectives in your plan have been confirmed by the customer sponsor — not inferred from internal discussion.
✓
The team understands the customer's business, not just their tech stack.

Value Definition

Core Question What will we do for our customer, and how will we win together?

What good looks like in Definition

By the end of Phase 2, your plan should pass these three tests.

✓
The shared vision reads like one story, not five disconnected pitches.
A customer reading the plan sees one Cisco — not a series of products competing for attention.
✓
Every initiative has a clear owner and a defined path.
It’s documented in the “Value Definition” tab in SalesMain and directly linked to driving new or expanded pipeline opportunities.
✓
Cross-architecture handoffs are surfaced before commit, not after.
Integration risks, dependencies, and adoption capacity questions are addressed before we present to the customer.
